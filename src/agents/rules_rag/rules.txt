# Travel Invoice Validation Business Rules

## General Policy
All travel expenses must comply with company travel policy and be pre-approved through the proposal system.

## Employee Level Classification
- Level A: Senior Management (Directors, VPs, C-Suite)
- Level B: Middle Management (Managers, Senior Managers)
- Level C: Junior Management (Team Leads, Supervisors)
- Level D: Staff (Associates, Analysts, Individual Contributors)

## Fare Limits by Employee Level

### Domestic Travel (within country)
- Level A: Maximum fare limit ₹15,000 per trip
- Level B: Maximum fare limit ₹10,000 per trip
- Level C: Maximum fare limit ₹7,500 per trip
- Level D: Maximum fare limit ₹5,000 per trip

### International Travel
- Level A: Maximum fare limit ₹75,000 per trip
- Level B: Maximum fare limit ₹50,000 per trip
- Level C: Maximum fare limit ₹35,000 per trip
- Level D: Maximum fare limit ₹25,000 per trip

## Travel Class Restrictions
- Level A: Business class permitted for flights over 4 hours, economy otherwise
- Level B: Premium economy permitted for international flights, economy for domestic
- Level C and D: Economy class only for all travel

## Booking Requirements
- All travel must be booked at least 7 days in advance for domestic, 14 days for international
- Last-minute bookings require special approval from department head
- Employees must use company-approved travel vendors when possible

## Invoice Requirements
- Invoice must clearly show: passenger name, origin, destination, travel date, fare amount
- Invoice date must match or precede the travel date
- Passenger name must match employee name in proposal exactly
- Origin and destination must match approved itinerary

## Reimbursement Conditions
- Fare exceeding approved limit by more than 10% requires additional justification
- Cancellation fees are not reimbursable without valid reason
- Service fees and taxes are included in fare limit calculation
- Multiple segments on same trip are summed for total fare

## Validation Rules
1. APPROVED if:
   - Employee found in proposal with valid level
   - Fare is within or at limit for employee level
   - All required invoice fields are present and valid
   - Passenger name matches employee (allowing minor spelling variations)

2. REJECTED if:
   - Fare exceeds employee level limit by more than 10%
   - Employee not found in proposal
   - Missing critical information (name, date, fare, route)
   - Invoice appears fraudulent or tampered
   - Travel dates are in the distant past (>90 days) without explanation

3. MANUAL REVIEW REQUIRED if:
   - Fare exceeds limit by 5-10%
   - Name matching is ambiguous (multiple employees with similar names)
   - Invoice quality is poor and extraction confidence is low
   - Employee level is not specified in proposal
   - Unusual route or fare pattern detected

## Special Circumstances
- Emergency travel may exceed limits with proper documentation
- Training and conferences may have different fare limits
- Client-billed travel follows client's policy, not internal policy
- Group bookings require separate approval process

## Fraud Detection
- Flag if fare amount appears altered or inconsistent
- Flag if invoice date is after travel date
- Flag if same invoice submitted multiple times
- Flag if passenger name doesn't match any employee reasonably

## Data Quality Standards
- Extraction confidence below 70% triggers manual review
- Missing fare limit in proposal requires fallback to lowest level
- Ambiguous dates should default to ISO format interpretation
- Currency conversions must use date-specific exchange rates
