COMPANY TRAVEL POLICY — BUSINESS RULES (DUMMY DATA)

====================================================
SECTION 1 — EMPLOYEE LEVEL DEFINITIONS
====================================================
L1 – Entry Level / Intern
L2 – Associate
L3 – Senior Associate
L4 – Manager
L5 – Director / Executive Leadership (Top Level)

====================================================
SECTION 2 — TRAVEL MODE POLICY & MAXIMUM REIMBURSEMENT
====================================================

[Cab / Taxi Fare Limits]
- L1: Maximum claim allowed ₹250 per trip
- L2: Maximum claim allowed ₹350 per trip
- L3: Maximum claim allowed ₹450 per trip
- L4: Maximum claim allowed ₹600 per trip
- L5: Maximum claim allowed ₹1000 per trip

[Auto-Rickshaw]
- All levels: Max claim ₹150 per trip

[Train Ticket Limits]
- L1: Sleeper class only, max reimbursement ₹500 per single trip
- L2: Sleeper or 3AC based on manager approval, max ₹1000
- L3: 3AC mandatory, max ₹1500
- L4: 2AC allowed, max ₹2500
- L5: Business 2AC / First Class allowed, no strict fare cap (soft cap ₹3500)

[Flight Ticket Limits]
- L1: Flights not permitted except special approval
- L2: Only Economy class, max reimbursement ₹5000
- L3: Economy class, max reimbursement ₹8000
- L4: Economy or Premium Economy, max ₹12000
- L5: Business Class allowed, soft cap ₹25000

====================================================
SECTION 3 — ACCOMMODATION (HOTEL) POLICIES
====================================================

Hotel Stay Maximum Rate Per Night:
- L1: ₹800
- L2: ₹1200
- L3: ₹1800
- L4: ₹2500
- L5: ₹4500

Common Rules:
- Hotel receipts must be itemized.
- Alcohol charges are not reimbursable.
- Late checkout fees allowed only if travel document supports late return.

====================================================
SECTION 4 — MEAL & DAILY ALLOWANCES
====================================================

Meal Allowance Per Day:
- L1: ₹250
- L2: ₹300
- L3: ₹400
- L4: ₹600
- L5: ₹800

General Meal Rules:
- Snacks are not considered meals.
- Bills must contain GST number and date.

====================================================
SECTION 5 — SPECIAL TRAVEL RULES
====================================================

One-Day Return Policy:
- If total travel duration is less than 24 hours, hotel stay cannot be claimed.
- Only local travel and meals can be reimbursed.
- For same-day return, only one meal allowance applies.

Maximum Business Trip Budget:
- L1: ₹1200 per day
- L2: ₹2000 per day
- L3: ₹3000 per day
- L4: ₹5000 per day
- L5: ₹10000 per day (flexible)

====================================================
SECTION 6 — GENERAL POLICIES
====================================================

- Travel must match approved itinerary from proposal.
- Name on invoice must match employee records.
- Travel date must be within ±3 days of approved proposal.
- Claims beyond limits require manager approval.
- Duplicate invoices are strictly rejected.
- Missing GST or missing ticket number reduces confidence score.
- Hand-written bills are accepted only for cab/auto with company stamp.

====================================================
SECTION 7 — EXCEPTIONS
====================================================

- Medical emergency travel automatically shifts validation to "pending approval".
- Weather delays or train cancellations can exceed budget by 20%.
- If employee level information is missing, default to L3 for calculation.
